ComfyUI-MaterialSync is now public.
It’s a custom ComfyUI node that sends generated PBR textures directly to Blender , Maya , and Unreal Engine , automatically creating and assigning materials.
Current features:
Blender support
Maya support
Unreal Engine support
Automatic PBR map detection
Automatic material creation & texture assignment
One-click sync from ComfyUI
The goal is to eliminate the repetitive export/import workflow and make AI-generated materials fit naturally into existing DCC and game engine pipelines.
Still actively developing it, so I’d love to hear feedback, ideas, or feature requests.
