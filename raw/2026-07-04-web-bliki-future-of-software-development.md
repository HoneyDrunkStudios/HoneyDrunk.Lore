# bliki: Future Of Software Development

Source: https://martinfowler.com/bliki/FutureOfSoftwareDevelopment.html

[Future Of Software Development](https://martinfowler.com/FutureOfSoftwareDevelopment.html)

2 July 2026

Thoughtworks hosted a pair of [Open Space](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/OpenSpace.html) workshops
called âThe Future of Software Developmentâ. They bring together various
Thoughtworkers and friends of Thoughtworks to exchange observations and
analysis for a day and a half on how the rise of AI and LLMs would affect our
profession.

I haven't attempted to create any kind of cohesive narrative of these events, after all I could only attend a fraction of the parallel sessions. Instead I've posted various insights in my fragments posts. This page lists these, together with posts from other attendees, and a more comprehensive report prepared by my colleagues.

These workshops were held under the [Chatham House
Rule](https://www.chathamhouse.org/about-us/chatham-house-rule), which allows us to report what was said, but not attribute it,
unless I received specific permission.
