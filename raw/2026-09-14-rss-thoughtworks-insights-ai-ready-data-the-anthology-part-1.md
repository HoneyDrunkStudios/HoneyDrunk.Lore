# AI-ready data: The anthology - Part 1

Source: [AI-ready data: The anthology - Part 1](https://www.thoughtworks.com/insights/blog/machine-learning-and-ai/ai-ready-data-part-1)

## Attributed article summary

Thoughtworks frames AI-ready data as an extension of foundational data engineering. Moving information to a cloud platform makes it available but does not resolve inconsistent meaning, poor quality, or weak governance.

Practical foundations include deterministic normalization of missing values and formats, classification by sensitivity and risk, least-privilege access, and checks on freshness and lineage. Metadata and catalogs make data discoverable and maintainable, while a semantic business layer resolves ambiguous names and department-specific definitions.

The article emphasizes funding and maintaining these foundations alongside visible AI features. Otherwise, retrieval and generation operate on unclear or untrusted inputs, making reliable downstream answers harder to produce.

HoneyDrunk relevance: source provenance, consistent metadata, explicit meaning, and access controls belong in ingestion and retrieval design. This practitioner article offers architectural guidance through analogies and examples; it does not provide a controlled benchmark of resulting AI quality.
