# How to Write an Effective Software Design Document

Source: [How to Write an Effective Software Design Document](https://refactoringenglish.com/excerpts/write-an-effective-design-doc/)

## Attributed content summary

Michael Lynch explains design documents as a way to expose costly decisions before implementation and collect useful feedback. Investment should scale with complexity, lifespan, risk, and coordination needs; a short solo project may require little documentation.

His main filter is the cost of choosing incorrectly. Hard-to-reverse decisions deserve design review, while cheap interface adjustments generally do not merit extensive debate. A document should provide enough context to stand alone for readers who missed prior conversations.

Suggested components include a clear objective, background and evidence, related documents, outcome-oriented goals, explicit exclusions, and concrete scenarios. The article supplies an example design document and further guidance for review.

For HoneyDrunk architecture work, this is a practical structure for distinguishing durable decisions from implementation details and making assumptions visible across people and agents. It is practitioner guidance rather than evidence that a fixed template or document length improves every project.
