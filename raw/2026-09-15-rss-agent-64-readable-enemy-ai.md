## Attributed content summary

Replicant D6 describes the enemy design of Agent 64: Spies Never Die, a Unity FPS with custom game logic. The goal is readable combat that evokes late-1990s console shooters rather than maximizing tactical intelligence.

Enemies perceive sight and sound and can taunt, attack, or seek allies. Perception and rallying are limited to avoid a single encounter alerting the entire map. Exaggerated motion and visible reaction time help players recognize danger and respond before taking damage.

Objectives and difficulty can change enemy priorities or allegiances, creating variation beyond increasing health or damage. The author emphasizes some unpredictability within understandable behavior and deliberately tuned timing.

The interview offers reusable encounter and feedback principles for a small game team. It does not disclose the author's state-machine implementation or provide performance measurements. Treat its claims as one developer's design account, and validate readability through playtests rather than assuming that more complex enemy logic improves the experience.
