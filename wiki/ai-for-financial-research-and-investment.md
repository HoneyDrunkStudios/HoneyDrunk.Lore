# AI for Financial Research and Investment

## Decision-useful summary
AI can accelerate investment research, but the durable advantage described by the Thoughtworks source comes from better systems: clean data plumbing, validated differentiated signals, counter-consensus hypothesis testing, citation-grade evidence, execution-aware portfolio construction, and human judgment. Speed alone is treated as table stakes, not alpha. [source: raw/2026-08-22-rss-thoughtworks-insights-the-alpha-playbook-ai-for-investment-professiona.md]

## Source-backed claims
- Thoughtworks argues that faster filing summaries do not create defensible alpha once many firms can process the same public information quickly; differentiation shifts toward proprietary data, better interpretation, validation, and execution. confidence: 1 Thoughtworks practice source, last-confirmed 2026-08-22. [source: raw/2026-08-22-rss-thoughtworks-insights-the-alpha-playbook-ai-for-investment-professiona.md]
- The source frames modern factor research around price momentum, fundamental momentum, and narrative momentum, with AI/NLP pipelines turning unstructured text into candidate quantitative features that still require out-of-sample tests, bias controls, regime checks, turnover, and transaction-cost analysis. confidence: 1 source, last-confirmed 2026-08-22. [source: raw/2026-08-22-rss-thoughtworks-insights-the-alpha-playbook-ai-for-investment-professiona.md]
- The source says AI can act as an automated data steward through schema mapping, deterministic calculation services, and anomaly/reconciliation audits, but it should not hallucinate missing financial inputs. confidence: 1 source, last-confirmed 2026-08-22. [source: raw/2026-08-22-rss-thoughtworks-insights-the-alpha-playbook-ai-for-investment-professiona.md]
- The source emphasizes citation-grade RAG for analyst workflows: claims in research outputs must trace to specific source evidence, and prompt/context design should limit permitted sources, task scope, evidence, definitions, output schema, and decision constraints. confidence: 1 source, last-confirmed 2026-08-22. [source: raw/2026-08-22-rss-thoughtworks-insights-the-alpha-playbook-ai-for-investment-professiona.md]

## Explicit relationships
- AI research pipelines depend-on clean data, governed calculations, source attribution, and validation before signals become investment decisions.
- Counter-consensus agents complement summarization agents by challenging assumptions rather than compressing consensus text.
- Execution-aware portfolio construction complements thesis generation because transaction costs, liquidity, and market impact can erase paper alpha.
- Citation-grade RAG depends-on task scope, permitted sources, output schema, and exact evidence links.
